# Hi, I'm Johnny!
Welcome to my Innovator Journal! I'm Johnny, a student at The Webb School who is interested in technology, computer science, engineering, and creative projects. I enjoy learning how things work and then trying to build or improve them myself.
## About Me
I am especially interested in **computer science and technology**. I like working on projects where I can solve problems, experiment with different ideas, and create something that actually works. Recently, I've been learning about things like Arduino, programming, and other technology-related projects.
Outside of technology, I enjoy playing sports, especially volleyball and tennis. I also enjoy filmmaking, video editing, gaming, and spending time with my friends.
## Experiences That Have Shaped Me
One of the biggest experiences that has shaped me was moving from China to the United States and attending boarding school. Before coming to Webb, I lived in Shenzhen for most of my life. Moving to a new country and joining a new school pushed me outside of my comfort zone.
At Webb, I have had to become more independent and learn how to manage my time between academics, sports, activities, and personal interests. These experiences have helped me become more willing to try new things and learn from challenges.
## What Inspires Me
I am inspired by people who take an idea and turn it into something real. I think it is exciting when technology can be used to solve everyday problems or create something that people can actually use.
I am also motivated by curiosity. When I don't understand how something works, I usually want to figure it out rather than just move on. I enjoy the process of experimenting, making mistakes, and improving my ideas.
## My Goals
One of my main goals is to become better at **computer science and engineering** and eventually use those skills to create useful projects.
I also want to become a better problem solver and learn how to work effectively with other people. In the future, I hope to build projects that combine technology with creativity and have a real impact on people's lives.
For now, my goal is to keep learning, keep experimenting, and keep building.
